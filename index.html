Evolutionary genomics, statistical genetics, and human disease

I am a researcher at the University of California, Irvine. I work on new gene evolution, population genetics, 3D genome organization, GWAS, and Mendelian randomization, connecting genome evolution to medical, agricultural, and conservation problems.

Notices

Updates

August 2026

Disease prevalence and reproductive fitness submitted to Nature Genetics

Work asking why some human diseases are common and others rare, through the coupling between disease-associated genetic variation and reproductive fitness. A preprint is in production at Research Square.

August 2026

“Out of South Asia” wild boar paper accepted by Science

A project started in 2014 on South Asian wild boars and domestic pigs has been accepted by Science. It presents evidence for an Out of South Asia origin of global wild boar populations. Data are on Zenodo and the pipeline is documented in the GitHub Wiki.

May 2026

New work at UCI: maize 3D genomics

At UCI I am working on maize 3D genome organization, using newly generated data from the maize NAM population to study population-level variation in genome architecture.
